iPhone 15 Pro Max

The iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro Max are
smartphones that were developed and marketed by Apple.
They are the seventeenth-generation flagship
iPhones, succeeding the iPhone 14 Pro and 14 Pro Max.
Similar to the iPhone 15, the 15 Pro replaced the proprietary
Lightning connector with USB-C.
They also support Apple Intelligence, which was introduced with iOS 18.
